About us

Who we are

A media and broadcasting company from Birtamode, Jhapa, on air and in the community since 2010.

The company

Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd.

Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd. is a leading media and telecommunications company established on 14 April 2010, with the vision of delivering reliable information, quality broadcasting, and meaningful communication to society.

Located at Birtamode-4, Jhapa, Nepal, the company has been actively contributing to the media sector through its commitment to public awareness, entertainment, and community engagement.

Our station

Radio Sunrise 90.2 MHz

Under Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd., Radio Sunrise 90.2 MHz operates as a trusted broadcasting platform, serving listeners with news, information, educational content, entertainment programs, and community-focused initiatives.

Over the years, Radio Sunrise has built a strong connection with audiences and has become an important voice within the region.

  • On air daily on 90.2 MHz FM since 2010
  • Listeners across Jhapa and 15 more districts
  • News, talk shows, music, and public service programming
  • Trusted by more than 900 clients across the region
