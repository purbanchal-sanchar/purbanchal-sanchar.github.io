Privacy Policy

Chat Story Maker, an Android app by Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd.
Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Chat Story Maker app ("the app") handles, why, and what choices you have. Chat Story Maker lets you write funny made-up chat stories, drawn as a comic, a notebook or a retro game. It has no accounts and no sign-in.

Information that stays on your device

  • Your chats. Character names, status lines and the lines you write are saved in a database inside the app on your phone. The app never sends them to us or anyone else, and we cannot see them.
  • Character photos. If you pick a photo for a character, the app copies a small version of it into its own private storage on your phone. The app never sends it to us or anyone else.
  • Saved images. Images you save go to your phone's Pictures folder. Images you share go only to the app you choose in the share menu.
  • Settings. Your theme choice and similar preferences are stored on your phone.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's chats, photos and settings in your own Google account backup, so they can be restored to a new phone. That backup is yours, managed by Google under your account settings, and we have no access to it.

You can delete any chat inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, except images you saved to your gallery, which you can delete from your gallery app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location,
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change that choice any time from Settings, Privacy choices in the app. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy .

App configuration

When the app starts it downloads a small settings file from our website over a secure (HTTPS) connection, to check for required updates. As with any web request, the server that hosts our site receives your IP address. We do not use it to identify you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not read your contacts, messages, call logs or location.
  • We do not receive your chats or photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Everything the app stores stays in its private storage on your phone. Network requests made by the app and by the ads SDK use encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
