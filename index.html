How we work

Four steps from brief to broadcast

A short, clear process, whether you are booking a single spot or a season of programming.

STEP 01

Tell us the message

We start with who you want to reach and what you need them to hear. Budget and timing come into the conversation early, not at the end.

STEP 02

Write and record

Our production team writes the script, records the voice, and cuts the audio in our own studio. You hear it before anyone else does.

STEP 03

Schedule the airtime

We place your spot in the slots your audience actually listens to, whether that is the morning news, the evening request hour, or a festival special.

STEP 04

Go on air and report back

Your campaign runs on 90.2 MHz, and you get a clear record of when it aired so you know exactly what you paid for.

Who we are

A voice for eastern Nepal since 2010

Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd. was established on 14 April 2010 with a simple aim: reliable information, quality broadcasting, and communication that means something to the people who hear it.

From our base at Birtamode-4 in Jhapa, Radio Sunrise 90.2 MHz has grown into a trusted voice in the region, carrying news, education, entertainment, and the concerns of the communities it serves.
