Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This policy explains how Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us") handles information on this website. It applies to this site only. Anything you send us by email or tell us over the phone is covered by the section on contacting us below.

The short version

  • This site has no accounts, no logins, and no sign-up.
  • We do not set advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • The only information you give us is what you choose to put in an email or a phone call.

Information this website collects

This is a static website. It has no contact form and no database, so nothing you do here is stored by us. The company that hosts the site keeps standard server logs, which typically include your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the time of the request. Those logs exist to keep the site running and secure, and we do not use them to identify individual visitors.

Fonts and external resources

Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google describes how it handles that data in its privacy policy. We receive nothing from those requests.

Contacting us

If you email us at the address on our contact page, we keep your message and your contact details for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of any booking or agreement that follows. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not pass your details to anyone else unless the law requires it.

Advertising bookings

If you book advertising or sponsorship with us, we hold the business details needed to run and invoice that campaign, including your name, organization, contact details, and the content you asked us to broadcast. We keep those records for as long as our tax and broadcasting obligations in Nepal require.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Write to us at the email address on our contact page and we will respond as quickly as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.
