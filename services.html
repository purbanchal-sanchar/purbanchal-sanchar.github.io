Our services

FM radio broadcasting

Radio Sunrise 90.2 MHz is our flagship broadcasting platform, serving listeners across eastern Nepal with reliable news, entertainment, educational content, and community-focused programs.

Operating under Purbanchal Sanchar Pvt. Ltd., we reach local communities through quality FM transmission every day of the year.

Key features
  • Broadcast on 90.2 MHz FM frequency
  • Coverage across Jhapa and surrounding districts
  • Daily news, talk shows, and music programming
  • Educational and public awareness content
  • Reliable on-air presence since 2010
