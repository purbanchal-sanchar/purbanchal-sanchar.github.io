Privacy Policy

Virtual Balance Transfer Alert
Effective date: 2026-10-29

This privacy policy applies to the Virtual Balance Transfer Alert app for Android , published on Google Play by Purbanchal Sanchar. It explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

What the app is

Virtual Balance Transfer Alert is a coin simulator made just for fun. It shows notifications on your own phone about imaginary coins from made-up banks and senders. The coins have no value and no real money ever moves. The app does not connect to any bank, wallet or payment service, does not ask for any bank, card or payment details, and has no user accounts or sign-in.

Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone:

  • The alerts you create: the number of imaginary coins, the sender, bank and note you picked from the app's built-in lists, and when the alert was created, scheduled and shown.
  • Your settings, such as alert sound, vibration and your chosen waiting times.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. You can delete alerts at any time from the History screen, and you can remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data in your device backup to your own Google account, as it does for most apps. This is controlled by you in your device settings and is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see these backups.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts, and we have no server that receives your data. The only third party that receives information through the app is the advertising service described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version and language
  • Information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, then Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.

Update check

When the app opens, it downloads a small settings file from our website (purbanchal-sanchar.github.io) to check whether your version needs an update. No personal information is sent with this request. Our website is hosted by GitHub, which may record your IP address as part of normal web server logs, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show the coin alerts you create. If you decline, the app still works and keeps your alerts in History.
  • Run at startup: to set your scheduled alerts again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: to vibrate when an alert appears, if you turn vibration on.
  • Internet: to show ads and to run the update check described above.

The app does not access your contacts, messages, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone. Information is only processed by Google for advertising as described above.

International processing

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own, using the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Security

The app's network connections use encrypted HTTPS. Your alerts and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

Data retention

Alerts and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Google keeps advertising information for the periods described in its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect or hold personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through the tools above: deleting data in the app, changing your ad choices in Settings, or managing your advertising ID in your device settings. For information held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy. You can also contact us at the address below and we will help.

Children

The app is not designed for or directed at children, and it is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Earlier versions are available on request.
