Privacy Policy

Virtual Bank Balance Simulator
Effective date: 2026-10-29

This privacy policy applies to the Virtual Bank Balance Simulator app for Android, published on Google Play by Purbanchal Sanchar. It explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

What the app is

Virtual Bank Balance Simulator is a game about feeling rich. You add game coins to a vault, spend them, set the vault to any number, buy silly items in a shop, collect card skins and rank up. Game coins exist only inside the app. They are not real money, cannot be bought or cashed out, and cannot pay for anything. The app does not connect to any bank, wallet or payment service, does not ask for any bank, card or payment details, has no in-app purchases, and has no user accounts or sign-in.

Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps the following on your phone:

  • Your game progress: the coins in your vault, the coin log (amounts, the notes you typed and when each entry happened), the items you bought in the shop and the card skins you own.
  • The player name you choose, if you enter one. It is only shown inside the app and can be anything you like.
  • Your settings, such as whether coin drops are on, whether you finished the welcome screens, when an item was unlocked, and when the app last asked you for a rating.

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. You can delete coin log entries at any time, erase everything with Settings, then Reset everything, or remove it all by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data in your device backup to your own Google account, as it does for most apps. This is controlled by you in your device settings and is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot see these backups.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts, and we have no server that receives your data. If you rate the app with three stars or fewer, your rating stays on your phone and is not sent anywhere. The only third party that receives information through the app is the advertising service described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. This includes ads shown while the app opens, between screens, inside some screens, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to unlock a shop item or card skin. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version and language
  • Information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, then Privacy options. On any Android phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.

Update check

When the app opens, it downloads a small settings file from our website (vibeit-tech.github.io) to check whether your version needs an update and to read a few app settings, such as how long rewarded unlocks last. No personal information is sent with this request. The website is hosted by GitHub, which may record your IP address as part of normal web server logs, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to tell you when a coin drop lands in your vault. Coin drops can be turned off in Settings. If you decline the permission, the app still works and coin drops still appear in your coin log.
  • Internet and network state: to show ads and to run the update check described above.
  • Background work (wake lock, foreground service): used by Android's WorkManager to deliver scheduled coin drops on your phone. Nothing is sent off the device.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, messages, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone. Information is only processed by Google for advertising as described above.

International processing

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own, using the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Security

The app's network connections use encrypted HTTPS. Your game progress and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

Data retention

Your game progress and settings stay on your device until you reset them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Google keeps advertising information for the periods described in its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect or hold personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through the tools above: resetting data in the app, changing your ad choices in Settings, or managing your advertising ID in your device settings. For information held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy. You can also contact us at the address below and we will help.

Children

The app is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Earlier versions are available on request.
